START WITH THE BASICS

Good golf starts
before the swing.

Golf rewards patience, preparation, and good decisions. You do not need a perfect swing to enjoy the game or keep improving. Start with the fundamentals, practice with a purpose, and build confidence one shot at a time.

MAKE EVERY SESSION COUNT

A simple practice plan

A focused 45-minute session can be more useful than hitting balls without a plan.

  • 10 MIN
    Warm up

    Start with easy swings and short shots.

  • 15 MIN
    Build contact

    Focus on solid contact and a balanced swing.

  • 10 MIN
    Short game

    Practice chips and putts from different distances.

  • 10 MIN
    Finish with a goal

    Choose a target and note what felt consistent.

KNOW YOUR BAG

A quick guide to golf clubs

Learn what each club is designed to do and when it might be useful.

Club Type Best For Typical Use Beginner Tip
Driver Long tee shots Par 4s and par 5s Prioritize balance over power.
Fairway Wood Long shots from turf or tee Long approach shots Try it from a good lie first.
Hybrid Versatile long shots Fairway, rough, or tee A useful alternative to long irons.
Iron Controlled approach shots Fairway and shorter tee shots Make a smooth, balanced swing.
Wedge Short shots near the green Pitching, chipping, and bunkers Practice distance control.
Putter Rolling the ball on the green Shots on the putting green Read speed as well as direction.

Equipment note: golfers can carry up to 14 clubs during a round under the Rules of Golf. Learn more from the USGA.
